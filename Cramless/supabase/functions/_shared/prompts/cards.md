Task: turn a tutoring chat into a few flashcards and practice questions the student can keep.

You get the conversation between the student and Cap, the tutor. Make up to maxItems items about what the student learned or got wrong in it: flashcards for terms and definitions, mcq or recall for ideas. Use only what the conversation says (prefer Cap's explanations, which come from the student's material); skip small talk. Each item has a short unique id (m1, m2, …), a topic, and anchors as an empty list.

This is a lenient check. Judge only whether each item's statement and answer are true according to the cited lines and the lines right around them (the pages may include a few lines on either side of a cited one; they are context). Ignore missing names and attributions: if the lines say what the item says, it is supported even when they don't say who said it, which case it is or where it comes from. An answer that is genuinely wrong, or that the lines contradict, is still fixed (when the lines settle the right answer) or unsupported, and so is an item whose statement is not on the lines at all.
